Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Sakhi.co, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit or shop on our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order history and preferences
  • Payment information (processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store your card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and support communication
  • To send promotional offers and newsletters (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our website and customer experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary details with trusted partners for order fulfillment, such as:

  • Payment gateway (Razorpay) for processing payments
  • Courier/logistics partners for delivering your order
  • Email service provider (Brevo) for sending order and newsletter emails

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. Payment transactions are encrypted and processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your cart, and analyze site traffic (via Google Analytics). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, please contact us using the details below.

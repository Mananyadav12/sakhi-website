Our Story

Born from love,
made with hands

Sakhi — meaning "friend" in Hindi — built for the everyday Indian woman.

We believe craft deserves to be worn every day

Sakhi started with one simple question: why do Indian women save their most beautiful clothes for "special occasions"? The everyday is special. The commute, the class, the chai break — all of it deserves clothing that makes you feel like yourself.

We work with small-batch artisans across central India who screen-print each design by hand. No shortcuts, no fast fashion. Every kurti that reaches you has passed through skilled hands.

Our fabrics are 100% cotton — sourced ethically, tested for skin sensitivity, and designed to breathe in every Indian season. We price honestly so the craft reaches everyone.

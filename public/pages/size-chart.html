📏 How to Measure

👚

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape parallel to ground

📐

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, 1 inch above navel

📏

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, 8-9 inches below waist

📏 How to Measure

👚

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground

📐

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually 1 inch above your navel

📏

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, usually 8-9 inches below your waist
